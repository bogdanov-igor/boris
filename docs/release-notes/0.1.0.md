The first public release of Boris, a walrus in your Mac menu bar that keeps an eye on Claude.

- Sees every Claude session on your Mac: the terminal, VS Code and Cursor, Claude Desktop and Cowork, and other machines over SSH.
- Shows agents, subagents and workflows step by step, with the model each of them runs on.
- Lets you answer permissions, questions and plans from the menu bar, the notch or the mini window. This needs hooks, which are optional.
- Counts tokens, plan limits and the extra spend you really pay for.
- Explains what failed and why: a limit, an API error, the network.
- Tells you what matters with a Mac notification, a sound or a Telegram message.
- Comes with a walrus who has outfits, reactions and a few secrets.

Signed with Developer ID and notarized by Apple. From now on, updates arrive on their own.
